A firm structured around direct access

The firm was founded to provide dispute resolution, corporate and commercial, and conveyancing advice that stays close to the client throughout a matter. The partners work directly with clients rather than routing matters through layers of staff, and maintain associate relationships with firms elsewhere in East Africa for cross-border work.

Legal analysis is paired with commercial judgment — weighing regulatory, financial, and reputational considerations alongside the strict legal risk in a transaction or dispute.
