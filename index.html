Firm Philosophy

A firm structured around direct access and responsiveness

The firm was founded to provide dispute resolution, corporate and commercial, and conveyancing advice that stays close to the client throughout a matter — from first instructions through to trial or transactional completion.

The partners work directly with clients rather than routing matters through layers of administrative staff, ensuring that client instructions receive immediate strategic attention from senior counsel. In addition to our domestic practice across Kenya, we maintain active associate relationships with law firms throughout East Africa for cross-border transactions and multi-jurisdictional enforcement.

Matters are handled by combining rigorous statutory legal analysis with practical commercial judgment: understanding not only the legal risk in a transaction or dispute, but the regulatory, financial, and reputational considerations that sit alongside it.

Direct Partner Involvement

Both partners are personally engaged on client matters rather than delegating oversight entirely to junior staff. You consult and plan directly with Senior Counsel.

Regional East African Network

Associate firms across the East African Community (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and South Sudan) seamlessly support matters extending beyond Kenya's borders.

Multidisciplinary Approach

Legal advice is paired with commercial, regulatory, tax, and risk analysis appropriate to the matter, protecting business viability and asset values.
